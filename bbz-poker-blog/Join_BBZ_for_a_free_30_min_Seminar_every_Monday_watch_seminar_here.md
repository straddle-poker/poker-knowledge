Getting your week off to a good start is super important in poker. If you kick off your Mondays with good habits and solid study routines, you’re far more likely to continue with them throughout the rest of the week.
**Jordan “bigbluffzinc” Drummond** knows this better than anyone. That’s why he’s now offering a **free 30-minute poker training seminar** to everyone in the [BBZ Poker Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Mf5sDTd).
It’s called the BBZ Lecture Hall and school will be in session every **Monday at 9:30 am ET**.
That means Jordan is getting up nice and early (6:30 am his time) to ensure more people from around the world can get involved and improve their poker game.
“I’m going to be hanging out, talking poker, trying to provide some insight into strategies which will hopefully be helpful for people,” says Jordan.

So whether you’re looking to get better after a rough Sunday session or you’re gearing up for a big week of action, you can now enjoy some free poker coaching every Monday.
The free lecture hall kicked off on January 17, 2022, and so far it’s been a huge success with hundreds of attendees taking advantage of some free coach-facing material from one of the best poker coaches in the world.
