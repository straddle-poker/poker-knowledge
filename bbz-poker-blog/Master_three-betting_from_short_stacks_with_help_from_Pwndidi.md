There’s a special breed of annoyance in poker tournaments that often rears its head when you’re short-stacked: the player on your left who seemingly can’t resist 3-betting you at every opportunity.
It’s a frustrating situation, one that can chip away at your confidence and stack if not handled correctly. It’s particularly aggravating in live tournaments when you’re sat right next to the person hellbent on playing against you.
But there are strategies to combat this incessant aggression and turn the tables in your favor. Not only that, you can master 3-betting from short stacks yourself, so it’s you annoying others.
### Learn how to 3-bet effectively at short stacks
In his course for BBZ Poker, [**3-Betting at Shorter Stacks**](/product/3-betting-short/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=3-betting-short-stacks), high-stakes crusher Alex “Pwndidi” Theologis breaks down the significance of 3-betting, turning what seems like a simple action into a formidable weapon in your poker arsenal.
With his rich background of high-stakes victories and deep theoretical knowledge, Theologis has crafted a comprehensive guide aimed at equipping you with the skills needed to combat aggressive players and become one yourself.
The positional battles he covers include: UTG vs UTG+1, UTG vs HJ, UTG vs BTN, LJ vs HJ, LJ vs BTN, CO vs BTN, and BB vs SB.
Theologis’ roadmap is spread out over 19 chapters and 11+ hours of video content. You even get to see the man himself square off against GTOWizard.
“I think that’s fairly productive as you get to see me act in real-time and explain my thoughts,” says Theologis. “But most importantly, you get to see me punt on the things I’ve talked to you about!”
### Who is Alex “Pwndidi” Theologis?
Alex “Pwndidi” Theologis is one of the best no limit hold’em tournament players in the game today.
Don’t believe us? When drafting players for the inaugural PokerStars SCOOP League, team captain (and BBZ student) Lex Veldhuis declared he would have taken Theologis with a top pick if the draft was solely for no limit hold’em events.
Theologis has a staggering $13.8 million in online cashes plus almost $1.7 million won in live poker.
But here’s some info about the BBZ Poker coach you might not know. He grew up in Thessaloniki, Greece but now resides in the high-stakes haven of Vienna, Austria. Oh, and he used to work as a DJ.
We spoke to Theologis when he first joined the BBZ Poker team. [Get to know your new coach here](/alex-pwndidi-theologis-interview/), then check out his course.
