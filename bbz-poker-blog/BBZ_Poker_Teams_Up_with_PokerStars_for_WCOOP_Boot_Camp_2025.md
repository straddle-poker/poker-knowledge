Every year before the World Championship of Online Poker (WCOOP) on PokerStars, BBZ Poker partners with PokerStars to run the **WCOOP Boot Camp** , a warm-up series designed to help players prepare for one of the biggest online tournament festivals of the year.
The Boot Camp combines a full schedule of warm-up tournaments across multiple formats and buy-in levels with free coaching sessions led by the BBZ coaching roster. It is open to everyone, and the coaching sessions are completely free.
## WCOOP Boot Camp 2025
The 2025 WCOOP Boot Camp ran from August 3 to August 18, leading into the 24th WCOOP series (September 7 to October 1, 2025). Here is what was included.
Tournament schedule
137 tournaments across 69 unique events in Low and High buy-in tiers. Formats included No-Limit Hold’em, 8-Game, HORSE, Razz, and PL Badugi, with buy-ins ranging from $2.20 to $5,200 and satellites starting at $1.10. Combined guarantees exceeded $9 million.
The series wrapped up with two Main Events on August 17:
Main Event (High)
$1,050
$400,000 guaranteed
Main Event (Low)
$109
$600,000 guaranteed
Free coaching sessions
BBZ ran 12 free coaching sessions on August 1, 8, and 15 (10 AM to 2 PM ET each day), hosted on the [BBZ Discord server](https://discord.gg/ac7cKps). Sessions covered WCOOP-specific strategy, format preparation, and live Q&A. Recordings were made available afterward for anyone who could not attend live.

Leaderboard prizes
The Boot Camp Leaderboard awarded $60,000 in Power Passes to the top 100 players in tournaments with buy-ins up to $215:
1st to 4th
$10,300

5th to 8th
$2,500

9th to 100th
$109
Bronze Power Pass
Ticket Machine
The WCOOP Boot Camp Ticket Machine offered $150,000 in WCOOP tickets across two prize draws (August 12 and August 19). Players earned entries by completing challenges: play 10 Boot Camp events, or finish in the money in two events. Each draw awarded $75,000 in tickets.
