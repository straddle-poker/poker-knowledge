This guide teaches you how to export a custom report from PokerTracker 4 (PT4) and uploading it to the [MTT Database Leak Finder](https://bbzpoker.com/mtt-database-leak-finder/) on bbzpoker.com. Ensure your PT4 is updated for compatibility.
_Note: This tool works only with MTT hands from PT4. If you don’t have PT4, you can use their free trial version[here](https://www.pokertracker.com/)._
### 
### Step-by-Step
  1. Download the BBZ Custom [PT4 report file](https://bbzpoker.com/uploads/bbz_pt4_mtt_database_v1-1-pt4rpt.zip) (.zip) and unzip.
  2. Open PT4
  3. Go to “View Stats” → “T” → “My Reports”.
  4. Import the PokerTracker4 file you downloaded  
![](https://bbzpoker.com/uploads/pt4-import-1-v2.jpg)
  5. In PT4, choose the Report on the left hand side.
  6. Choose your desired date range.  
![](https://bbzpoker.com/uploads/pt4-import-2.jpg)
  7. Right-click under the stats area and select “Export Report”.
  8. Save the report file.
  9. Upload the saved report file to MTT Database Leak Finder.
