* * *
There’s no magic formula that only the best winning professionals have stumbled upon. There’s no secret tip they’ve been told that you haven’t.
The recipe for success in poker is hard work, study, execution, and consistency.
How do we know this? Because the proof is in the pudding.
Before they began studying with BBZ Poker, [**Fintan “easywithaces” Hand**](/product/fintan-bundle/) and **Ben “Spraggy” Spragg** were Twitch superstars and poker professionals with lots of experience battling through low to mid-stakes tournaments.
After two years of studying and coaching with BBZ Poker, they’re now both competing at high stakes, with great success.
Fintan has three SCOOP titles as well as a long list of big results:
  * 2020: $1,050 SCOOP Afternoon Deep Stack – $73,653
  * 2021: $5,200 SCOOP NLHE PKO High Roller – $232,156
  * 2022: $2,100 SCOOP Sunday Warm-Up PKO – $119,046

While Spraggy has two WCOOP titles and has enjoyed plenty of huge scores:
  * 2020: $10,300 SCOOP Main Event – $40,955
  * 2021: $530 Bounty Builder – $21,545
  * 2021: $1,050 WCOOP PKO Turbo – $67,969

They got better at poker the right way. Now you can too as you learn along with them.
Both Hand and Spragg have now released training bundles for BBZ Poker, each priced at just $99. Both bundles have important and unique content that will no doubt open your eyes to many strategies and concepts you hadn’t previously considered.
