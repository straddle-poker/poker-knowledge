The BBZ Poker Community Awards are designed to celebrate our students’ success stories and the best posts in [our Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Mf5sDTd).
We’ll be giving out three monthly awards: the Constant Crusher award, Like A Boss award, and the Best Mic Drop award.
Find out who won in July 2022 below.
* * *
### Constant Crusher Award
The Constant Crusher Award is precisely what it sounds like: a big shout-out to the person who consistently puts up great results throughout the month.
July’s winner?
**Schwibbs1.**
What more can we say about this guy that we haven’t already said?
When we spoke with [Alex “Schwibbs1” Schwint](/pascoop-champ-alex-schwibbs1-schwint-i-owe-all-of-my-success-to-bbz/) in May 2021, he’d been crushing the PASCOOP. And he had no plans of stopping.
“Even though the results in 2021 have been insane, I am trying to stay humble and hungry,” he [told us](/pascoop-champ-alex-schwibbs1-schwint-i-owe-all-of-my-success-to-bbz/). “There is still a TON of work I can put in on my game.”
He’s continued to put in the work studying with BBZ Poker, and as a result, he continues to get significant results.
This month, Schwibbs1 not only took down the WSOP.com PKO Weekly Thursday for $6,145:
![](https://bbzpoker.com/static/Screenshot-2022-07-29-113359-600x241.png)
He then went on to win a World Series of Poker bracelet!
![](https://bbzpoker.com/static/Screenshot-2022-07-29-113250-478x600.png)
The guy’s consistency is insane. So much so that this is the third time Schwibbs1 has won our Consistent Crusher award!
Keep it up.

### Like A Boss Award
The Like A Boss Award goes to someone from the BBZ community who pulled off a particularly impressive feat across the month.
July’s winner?
**MAK**.
After picking up the [BBZ Bundle](/product/bbz-bundle/) and all three [Quantitive Strategies videos](/poker-coaching-videos/), MAK got down to studying and saw results almost immediately.
He finished 15th in the GGPoker HR Main Event for $7,547, having qualified through a $55 satellite. Then he finished eighth in a $215 buy-in tournament on ACR for $3,931!
![](https://bbzpoker.com/static/Screenshot-2022-07-29-111956-600x363.jpg)
“Thanks, BBZ,” he said. “Your work is amazing, great clarity of the thoughts and simple language for us to grasp your concepts.”
Congrats MAK!
