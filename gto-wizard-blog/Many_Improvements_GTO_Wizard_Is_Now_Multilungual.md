Wizards, today we are releasing multiple improvements, and GTO Wizard is now multilingual!
## **New Solutions**
**MTT 8max** (No limps); 10BB, 12BB, 14BB, 17BB, 20BB, 25BB _(3–8 sizings, 0.3% accuracy, PREFLOP ONLY)_  
**MTT 8max**(With limps); 30BB _(3–8 sizings, 0.3% accuracy, PREFLOP ONLY)_
You may be wondering why we have two types of solutions, one without limps and one with limps. The answer is simple. Our MTT users requested it, and we understood that real games are often played without limps, even at short stacks. So while pure GTO uses limping a lot, we wanted to have solutions that you can study and be sure that they will fit your table’s game style.
## **Improvements**
  * GTO Wizard now speaks Chinese, Spanish, Russian, Thai, Portuguese, Italian, French, and Romanian
  * Added support for Cash game Heads-up hands for HH Analyzer
  * Added HU SnG General Aggregated reports
  * Added HU SnG Complex Aggregated reports
  * Ability to customize the color of any bet size/bet category
  * Ability to customize hotkeys to suit your needs
  * Added themes gallery
  * Added a poker glossary with an explanation of 365 poker terms
  * Practice mode now displays up to 9 action buttons– Showing all values in the hand matrix in the ranges tab (Range, EV, EQ, EQR)

Let’s take a more in-depth look at each improvement.
![Many improvements & GTO Wizard is now multilingual!](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/gto-wizard-is-now-multilingual-image-5.jpg)
##### **GTO Wizard Is Now Multilingual**
Today we are releasing GTO Wizard translated into Chinese, Spanish, Russian, Thai, Portuguese, Italian, French, and Romanian. Other languages are still in progress, but you can expect them soon. To change your language, click on the “Account” icon in the top-right menu and there, you can select your preferred language. If you would like to help us translate GTO Wizard into your language, please get in touch with GTO Wizard Matt on [our Discord](https://discord.gg/ht3QxrP?ref=blog.gtowizard.com).
##### **Added Support for Cash Game Heads-Up Hands for HH Analyzer**
You can now analyze your Cash game Heads-up hands! Click on the upload button and get your hands quickly analyzed, and see where you deviate from GTO. We will soon introduce this feature also for MTT, Spin & Go, and HU SnG hands. However, for HH Analyzer to work perfectly, we would like to get some hands examples from you. Please share with us a few hands from your favorite sites to add support for them. [Please submit them here.](https://forms.gle/THkEWmKiQ7SMdU2o7?ref=blog.gtowizard.com)
##### **Added HU SnG General & Complex Aggregated reports (free)**
Today we are also adding Aggregated reports for both variants of our HU SnG solutions. Select any spot and then click on the tab “Reports”. Don’t forget that you can use various filters and groupings for diving deep into strategies. They are available for flops and turns. Also, we still have an active promotion, and all features, including Aggregated reports, are for HU SnG entirely free now (also for spins and MTTs). To get a better idea about how to use Aggregated reports, we recommend you[ watch this video](https://youtu.be/D1vzP_nj77I?ref=blog.gtowizard.com).
![Many improvements & GTO Wizard is now multilingual!](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/gto-wizard-is-now-multilingual-image-2.jpg)
##### **Ability To Customize the Color of Any Bet Size/Bet Category + Added Themes**
Since you can group your bets into different categories (small, medium, large, overbet) or all of them together, it wasn’t possible to customize the colors of single bets rather than only the categories. That’s changing today since we allow you to set the color of any action you want using gradients. Open the settings using the “setting wheel” icon in the top-right menu and select the bet category you want to change the colors for. Then choose the color of the smaller bet and the biggest bet, and it will automatically generate a gradient. We have also reworked the Setting page, and now it’s called Themes. You can see there is only the default GTO Wizard theme, but we will add a gallery of your submitted themes on our Discord in the upcoming days. To get your theme shown there, please submit it on [our Discord server](https://discord.gg/ht3QxrP?ref=blog.gtowizard.com) in the #themes channel. We will post it there with your nickname, so everyone can see who made it. Don’t forget to name your theme as well!
![Many improvements & GTO Wizard is now multilingual!](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/gto-wizard-is-now-multilingual-image-4.jpg)
##### **Ability To Customize Hotkeys To Suit Your Needs**
If you didn’t know, you can use multiple hotkeys to navigate in GTO Wizard faster. Until today, all hotkeys were fixed, but since you requested to change them to suit you, even more, we have listened to you, and now it’s possible. Click in the top-right menu on the “Hotkeys” or [this link](https://app.gtowizard.com/user/hotkeys?ref=blog.gtowizard.com).
![Many improvements & GTO Wizard is now multilingual!](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/gto-wizard-is-now-multilingual-image-1.jpg)
##### **Added a Poker Glossary With an Explanation of 365 Poker Terms**
To start with learning GTO, it’s essential to understand basic poker terms, often used at many places in GTO Wizard. We have made a poker glossary with 365 most used poker terms, so that’s one term to learn every day for the next year! You can access the Poker Glossary in the app by clicking on the (…) menu next to the “Analyze” section or [this link](http://gtowizard.com/en/glossary/?ref=blog.gtowizard.com).
##### **Practice Mode Now Displays up to 9 Action Buttons**
Whenever there are six possible actions in the practice mode, we always show you a “betting slider,” so there is not a crazy amount of buttons. It also simulates the play on the actual poker site more accurately. However, while practicing with General solutions, there are often situations with 7–9 possible actions, and then the slider is ineffective. So from today, if there are nine possible actions to pick from, we will always show you nine buttons, and if there are more than nine possible actions, we will use the slider.
![Many improvements & GTO Wizard is now multilingual!](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/gto-wizard-is-now-multilingual-image-3.jpg)
##### **Showing All Values in the Hand Matrix in the Ranges Tab**
While browsing the ranges in the “Ranges” tab, we realized there is too much space in the hand matrix (the cell showing the details of your combo). So we added there all values for each combo (Range, EQ, EQR, and EV).
And that’s all for this week! You can expect other updates very soon! Thanks for reading these patch notes. GL at the tables!
