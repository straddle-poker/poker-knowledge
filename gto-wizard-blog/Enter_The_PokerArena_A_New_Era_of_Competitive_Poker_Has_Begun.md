## **Multiple Ways To Play. One Poker Ecosystem.**
{{width: 75%}}
[![Poker Arena](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/article-3-1.png)](https://gtowizard.com/pokerarena?ref=blog.gtowizard.com)
{{/width}}
**PokerArena has three core ways to play:**
  1. **Competitive** – Ranked [heads-up poker](https://gtowizard.com/play-mode/heads-up-poker?ref=blog.gtowizard.com) with scoring, leaderboards, and rewards. Precise poker play matters.
  2. **Casual** – Same gameplay, zero pressure. No scores, no stakes. Free to analyze and improve or play [1v1 poker](https://gtowizard.com/play-mode/1v1-poker?ref=blog.gtowizard.com) and have fun.
  3. **Play With Friends** – Host private games for up to 9 players. No sign-up is needed for guests. Just share a link and play some [poker with friends.](https://gtowizard.com/play-mode/poker-with-friends?ref=blog.gtowizard.com)

Competitive mode and Casual mode work on desktop, mobile browser, and iOS. All progress syncs. It’s full crossplay with a single account. Play With Friends is supported on desktop and mobile browsers (beta).
