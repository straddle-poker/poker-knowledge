## Navigation
Select the [Hands page](https://app.gtowizard.com/analyze/hands) using the “Analyze” dropdown menu at the top of the page. This will take you to your analyzed hands.
![Analyzed Hands](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-1.png)
Alternatively, you can access your hands by selecting a particular session from the Uploads page. Select the view icon![gto-wizard-view-icon-eyeball](https://blog.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/gto-wizard-view-icon-eyeball.png) on the right-hand side to jump to your analyzed hands. This will show you a hand history report containing hands only from that session.
[![Analyzed Hands](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-2.png)](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-2-1200x277.png)
You can also use this menu![Analyzed Hands](https://blog.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/gto-wizard-navigation-menu-tooltip.png) on the left-hand side of the Analyzer pages to navigate.
## Hand history table
The Hand History table is the primary display of the hand analysis feature. From here, you can organize, filter and sort your hands. You can quickly find your biggest EV losses. You can replay a hand, and dive into further detail using the hand history toolbar.
[![Analyzed Hands](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-3.png)](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-3-1200x682.png)
### Statuses
![Analyzed Hands](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-4.png)
**Analyzed**  
Your hand is fully analyzed.
**Non-existing GTO spot**  
Indicates you’ve run into a spot with no solution. This happens in multiway pots, situations where the stack sizes are too deep or shallow, or if the hand is in the wrong format. This can also indicate that the hand takes a line that is never supposed to be played, which may or may not be due to bet sizing discrepancies.
**Error**  
There was an error in your hand, in this case, please see more info in the article below or contact **[our Discord](https://discord.gg/ht3QxrP)**.
__
Please read about what formats and which poker sites we can analyze[here](https://blog.gtowizard.com/how-to-get-your-hand-histories/).

### Columns
The columns above the Hand History table can be used to sort your hands by any numerical value. You can sort by date, EV loss, BB W/L or pot.
[![Analyzed Hands](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-8.png)](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-8-1200x31.png)
We recommend sorting the table by EV loss in order to find your leaks and maximize your study time!

### Bet Sizing Discrepancies
The Analyzer works by comparing your hand history to a presolved solution from preflop to turn. The river is calculated using the exact board state (stacks/sizes/etc).
Bet sizes are matched to the closest available size within the solutions. However, discrepancies can still occur. These are shown in parentheses next to your actual bet size.
__
For example in this spot, CO actually opened 2.5BB, but the solution uses 2.3BB.
![Analyzed Hands](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-15.png)
You can change the solutions used to analyze your hands in the settings menu under Analyzer.  
For example, if you normally open 2.5x from early position, you can change the solution to General 2.5x.
[![Analyzed Hands](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-16.jpg)](https://help.gtowizard.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/analyzed-hands-image-16.jpg)
