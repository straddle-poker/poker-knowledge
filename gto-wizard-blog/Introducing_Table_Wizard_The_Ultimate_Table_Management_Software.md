### **Example:**
Have you ever looked over at a table and had your mind turn blank? What even happened, where are we, and how did we get here? It happens to everyone from time to time. This is where Table Wizard comes in.
Looking at the action replay, it becomes obvious what went down. This is a HJ vs SB SRP. Each street is separated by a · dot:
  * **Preflop** : HJ opened 2.3bb (R2.3), SB called (C)
  * **Flop** : SB checked (X), HJ bet 33% pot (B33%), SB raised 66% (R66%), HJ called (C)
  * **Turn** : SB checked (X), HJ checked (X)
  * **River** : SB bet 50% (B50), HJ went all in (AI 82%), action on SB
