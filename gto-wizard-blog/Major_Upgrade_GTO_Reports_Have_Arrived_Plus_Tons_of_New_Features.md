## **GTO Reports: Analyze, Compare, Improve**
****Instant Preflop Analysis****
GTO Reports directly compares your preflop stats to GTO benchmarks for cash games. This tool helps you establish solid preflop fundamentals by clearly visualizing where your play deviates from optimal strategy.
  * **Restrictions** – Currently available for **cash games only** and **preflop stats only**. Postflop stats and other formats are coming soon!

****Choose Your GTO Baseline****
We use high-quality solver solutions to generate accurate comparisons. Choose from:
  * 200bb or 100bb stack depths
  * NL500 or NL50 rake structures

****Core Preflop Stats Covered:****
Evaluate your preflop strategy across key stats, including VPIP, PFR, VPIP/PFR, RFI, Limp, Squeeze, 3-Bet, and 4-Bet, and 5-Bets.

Look at this **4-bet stat** —20.1%, compared to the GTO baseline of **15.5%**. That’s a significant gap, suggesting **{{color: red}}excessive aggression{{/color}}**. We see the same tendency for aggressive **3-betting** (13.1% vs a GTO baseline of 9.7%) and squeezing (14.1% vs 7.8%).
Here, you can see how GTO Reports visually highlight these imbalances: stats exceeding GTO are marked in **{{color: red}}red{{/color}},** while those falling short are **{{color: blue}}blue{{/color}}** , making it easy to spot leaks at a glance. Identifying these discrepancies is exactly why GTO Reports are such a valuable tool.
****Advanced Filtering for Targeted Study****
  * **Date Filters** – Isolate specific timeframes
  * **Values** – ‘Absolute’ displays your raw stat, whereas ‘Relative’ shows the difference between your stat and the GTO benchmark
  * **Deviation from GTO** – Highlights stats that are higher or lower than GTO

****Deeper Insights with Position-Based Breakdown****
Click any primary stat to reveal detailed analysis:
  * **Positional Breakdown** – See how your play varies by position
  * **Position vs. Position Matrix** – Analyze key matchups (e.g., CO vs. HJ)
  * **‘Vs’ Toggle** – Examine how you respond to opponent actions (e.g., facing a 3-Bet)

This tool provides a complete diagnostic of your preflop tendencies, giving you the insights needed to fine-tune your approach. Upload your hand histories today and take control of your leaks!

###### **Example: Breaking Down a 3-Bet Stat**
Clicking on the 3-bet stat reveals a positional breakdown, showing how often you 3-bet from each position compared to GTO. Below that, the position vs. position matrix provides a granular look at how you respond to an open raise from every position.
###### **Defending vs. 3-Bets**
Toggling the ‘Vs 3-Bet’ switch flips the view to analyze your defense strategy. Instead of seeing your 3-bet frequency, you’ll now see how often you fold, call, or raise when facing a 3-bet—broken down by position vs. position. This allows you to pinpoint exactly where your defense is too tight or too loose and adjust accordingly.
GTO Reports make it effortless to visualize where your frequencies deviate from optimal play, so you can make targeted adjustments and plug leaks fast.

## **Analyzer Improvements: Powerful New Tools for Deeper Hand Analysis**
We’ve completely revamped the Analyzer with a smoother, more intuitive interface and several powerful new features to help you pinpoint and fix leaks faster than ever.
****Postflop Action Sequence Filters****
Drill down into specific betting lines with precise postflop action sequence filtering. Want to review:
  * Every check-raise opportunity you faced?
  * All your delayed c-bets that went wrong?
  * Those crucial triple-barrel spots?
  * Any specific betting pattern that matters to your game?

Simply define the postflop betting line using our new action sequence filters and instantly access every matching hand in your database. This targeted approach helps you identify patterns in specific spots and develop more nuanced strategic adjustments.
For example, here we have filtered for spots where someone overbet probed the turn:

****Enhanced Analyzer Filters****
We’ve expanded our analyzer with additional powerful filters to refine your hand analysis even further. Now, alongside action sequences, you can quickly filter hands based on:
  * Whether Hero saw the flop, turn, or river
  * The opponent’s position
  * The {{tooltip-title: effective stack depth}}

Effective stack is measured as the shortest preflop stack between hero and VPIP’d opponents.

These enhanced filters allow for deeper analysis, enabling you to dissect your play with greater precision.

****Hand History Annotation System****
Context matters in poker decisions! Our new hand history annotation system lets you add custom notes and color-code hands for easier categorization and review.
Did you intentionally deviate from GTO against a recreational player? Tag it with a note explaining your exploitative adjustment. Working on a specific leak? Color-code related hands for your next study session. These annotations stay with your hand histories, creating a personalized study framework that evolves with your game.

****One-Click Stats-to-Hands Filtering****
We’ve streamlined the leak-finding workflow with direct connections between your stats and hand histories. When reviewing your aggregate statistics, simply click any stat value to instantly generate a filtered hand report for that specific scenario.
Notice your squeeze play showing a high error rate? One click on that stat cell immediately pulls up all relevant squeeze hands for review. This seamless integration between stats and hand histories eliminates tedious manual filtering, getting you straight to the hands that matter most.

This shortcut works for any aggregate stat table when filtering for **Preflop Action** , **Position** , or **Preflop Aggression**. It does not work when filtering by Street.
