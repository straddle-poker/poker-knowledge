Today, we are releasing our largest batch of new solutions to date! **In fact, we added so many new solutions that we needed to completely revamp the solution selector!**
This update includes an intuitive new interface for finding solutions and quickly switching between them! Now you can switch between depths or solution types while staying in the same spot.
But that’s not all. We also added the ability to save filters in reports, randomize/increment the board (_switch between the runouts with hotkeys_), a new Strategy+EQ view, and we’ve added several updates to the Trainer’s information panel.
We intend to add an extra MTT tier with more solutions in the future. This new tier will cover thousands of ICM and PKO spots. **Don’t worry, you won’t lose access to any solutions you’ve had up to this point.** Premium members can access the new ICM solutions in the meantime.

For **MTTs** , you can filter by stack distribution. This is especially important for ICM scenarios where different stack distributions can change your risk premium and strategy. We’ve categorized stack distributions into four groups: **Symmetric** , **Near** , **Big Stack** and **Far**. The goal is to group strategically-similar stack distributions to make studying easier.
{{width: 50%}}
![](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/2026/04/thousands-of-new-solutions-image-6-600x127.jpg)
{{/width}}
  * **Symmetric**  
Equal stack sizes. This means all players have the same stack depth. Symmetric stack depths tend to have less ICM pressure.
  * **Near**  
Stack depths are close to each other; between 1-3BB away from each other.
  * **Big Stack**  
One or two bigger stacks relative to the rest of the table. Big stacks have a lot of influence on ICM strategies as they can often bully the smaller stacks and pay a lower risk premium.
  * **Far**  
Stack depths vary more; between 3–7BB away from each other. Varied stack depths alter the risk premium and strategy between short/mid/large stacks.

****QuickSwitch Solutions****
Many Wizards value comparing and contrasting different types of solutions and stack depths. Previously, when you chose a new solution, you’d lose your spot in the tree and have to select all the actions, cards, and positions again. **That’s why we’ve added the new QuickSwitch feature!** This feature saves your spot in the tree, including the positions, board, and bet sizes. You can switch between depths/solutions using your mouse. Click the arrow underneath the solution selector to open the QuickSwitch menu:
![](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/2026/04/thousands-of-new-solutions-image-21.gif)
This is a quick and easy way to compare strategies in different solutions and stack depths. Note that some solutions have different bet sizes, so the nearest bet sizes (as a percentage of the pot) will be chosen. **We also added a hotkey to make this feature easier to use!** Press your keyboard’s up or down arrow to cycle through similar solutions and stack depths quickly!
![](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/2026/04/thousands-of-new-solutions-image-22.gif)

Select the **Save button** on the bottom left to save your filtering criteria.
