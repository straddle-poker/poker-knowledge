## How does it work?
[GTO Wizard](https://gtowizard.com/) will automatically select the closest rake structure and stack size based on the hand history information. Your play is compared against pre-solved solutions from preflop-turn; the river is calculated based on the exact stack depth and bet sizes. The flop-turn analysis will select sizes closest to the bet sizes used in the game.
The Analyzer will ignore certain hands for which we don’t have solutions – such as multiway postflop spots. Some hands have partial solutions, such as hands where you take an action that’s never played in the GTO Strategy.
