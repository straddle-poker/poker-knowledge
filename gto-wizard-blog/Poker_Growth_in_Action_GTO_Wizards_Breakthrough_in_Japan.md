GTO Wizard is a driving force in the global progression of poker, consistently expanding its influence and strengthening the poker community worldwide. Japan, a vibrant and evolving market, is a key part of our journey. We are proud to be actively involved in fostering poker’s growth in Japan. Not only are we dedicated to driving innovation in how the game is studied, but also to the growth and evolution of the poker industry as a whole. This dedication was exemplified in our recent activities in Japan.
## **POKER FORUM**
{{grid: 2}}
![Poker Forum 2024](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img-9359.jpg)![Poker Forum 2024](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/img-9360.jpg)

In 2024, GTO Wizard Japan launched Poker Forum, bringing poker enthusiasts from all over Japan to one place. For just $35, participants got to witness interactive poker seminars and panel discussions. Poker Forum 2024 also welcomed 2022 WSOP Main Event champion Espen Jørstad, now a regular in the world’s highest-stakes games. He spoke with attendees, answered questions, and engaged in thoughtful discussions with people from across the industry. Following the sold-out success of the inaugural 2024 Poker Forum at Tokyo Port City Takeshiba, we returned in 2025 with a bigger and bolder vision.

In June 2025, GTO Wizard Japan hosted **POKER FORUM 2025** at the Chunichi Building in Nagoya, one of the most exclusive venues in one of Japan’s most culturally significant hubs. With over 140 participants tuning in both in-person and online, this year’s forum built on the success of our 2024 debut, deepening the conversation around poker as a mindsport, with discussions around poker theory and solver technology incorporated.
The event featured expert presentations from leading voices in the Japanese poker community:
  * **Amu (GTO Wizard JP Coach & Ambassador) – MTT LIFE with GTO Wizard**  
How ICM study has evolved with new solver tools and where many players still misstep
  * **Wataru (GTO Wizard AI Expert) – The Progress and Future of GTO Wizard AI**  
A deep dive into QRE, ICM advancements, population-level analysis, and upcoming features
  * **Zaki – 20 Years of Poker Strategy**  
A historical tour of how preflop and c-bet strategies have evolved across eras
  * **Yogo – The Future of Solvers**  
What comes after solvers: adapting to player pools, field-level strategies, and real-world application

The event closed with a panel discussion and live Q&A, offering attendees the rare opportunity to speak directly with GTO Wizard’s engineers, researchers, and top strategists.
Would it really be a GTO Wizard event if we didn’t end it with a game of poker? Of course not. After a full day of sharp theory, deep panels, and lively discussion, attendees made their way to a separate venue for a catered networking lounge and an Ultra Poker–hosted tournament. The post-forum event attracted 160 entries, solidifying the forum’s transition from a knowledge summit to a true poker festival. It brought our vision full circle: connect, study, compete.
Two years, two cities, one collective goal: Advancing the mindsport of poker in Japan.

Known for its blend of sophistication, business, and culture, Akasaka provides the perfect backdrop for a space built around collaboration, learning, and elite-level poker discussion…
The lounge serves multiple purposes:

## **Collaborating With the Best of Japanese Poker**
GTO Wizard’s growth in Japan has been formed by strategic relationships with local talent. [Masato “World Wide” Yokosawa](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7MCAoLPEm6Q&ref=blog.gtowizard.com), with over 1 million YouTube subscribers, is the most-watched poker creator in the world, and a proud GTO Wizard Japan Ambassador. He has been on the biggest stages in world poker, whether it’s running deep in the European Poker Tour (EPT), World Series of Poker (WSOP) or playing high-stakes cash games on High Stakes Poker and other TV shows and streams.
Alongside Masato is [Amu](https://www.youtube.com/@GTOWizardJapan/?ref=blog.gtowizard.com), a respected GTO Wizard JP coach and one of Japan’s top educators. His work translating advanced solver concepts into actionable lessons has helped thousands of players deepen their understanding and results. Hand in hand, Amu has helped us grow the game in Japan by positioning poker as a mind sport.
Today, we have 18 partnered poker rooms across Japan where players can experience GTO Wizard hands-on. These rooms are supported by on-site staff trained in GTO Wizard and offer exclusive seminars, training events, and product support.
From the GTO Wizard Cup, which saw over 150 players compete, to national Japanese poker staples like the Japan Poker Festival and the Hyakka Ryouran Tournament, GTO Wizard is proud to support Japan’s most impactful and prestigious events. These initiatives and campaigns help connect strategic poker study to live competition, which draws thousands and reinforces our commitment to building a smarter, stronger, and more accepted poker ecology across Japan.
In 2025, we proudly welcomed Shiina Okamoto to Team GTO Wizard. Shiina has put together an extraordinary three-year run in the WSOP Ladies Championship, finishing runner-up in 2023, then winning the title back-to-back in 2024 and 2025. Her consistency and dominance on one of poker’s biggest stages make her a perfect fit for GTO Wizard’s mission to elevate the game through mastery, discipline, and innovation.
