****Short Stack****
These solutions provide preflop strategies tailored for niche game formats. The focus is on short-stack dynamics, such as GGPoker’s All-In or Fold, Winamax’s Short-Track, and America’s Cardroom’s 7 Day No Rathole.
#### **GG ALL IN OR FOLD**
**4-max** ; 1695 NEW Situations _(PREFLOP)_
  * Rake structures: ChipEV (no rake)
  * Stack depths: all available 4-player stack combinations, where one player always has 8bb. (3-8bb average stacks)

**3-max** ; 189 NEW Situations _(PREFLOP)_
  * Rake structures: ChipEV (no rake)
  * Stack depths: 3-player stack combinations, where one player always has 8bb. (3-8bb average stacks)

#### **Winamax Short-Track**
**5-max** ; 85 NEW Situations _(PREFLOP)_
  * Rake structures: ChipEV (no rake)
  * Stack depths: 5-20bb average stacks, with one player stack always 5bb

**6-max** ; 156 NEW Situations _(PREFLOP)_
  * Rake structures: ChipEV (no rake)
  * Stack depths: 10-30bb average stacks, with one player’s stack fixed to 10bb and the largest stack at the table 50bb
