Get ready to experience the next evolution in [online poker](https://gtowizard.com/play-mode/online-poker-game?ref=blog.gtowizard.com) with **GTO Wizard Play Mode** —a new way to play [poker with friends](https://gtowizard.com/play-mode/poker-with-friends?ref=blog.gtowizard.com) for free! Now, you can gather your friends, set up private games, and dive into a completely seamless experience, right from your browser. With no downloads, no sign-up, and no delays, the future of poker is at your fingertips. You can also try our [heads-up poker](https://gtowizard.com/play-mode/heads-up-poker?ref=blog.gtowizard.com) mode in which you can queue up to a 1v1 game and match your skill against random players of similar skill level.
Whether you’re playing for fun or polishing your skills, GTO Wizard Play Mode gives you everything you need to create, host, and enjoy poker games—anytime, anywhere. Just select the new **Play** tab in the top menu of GTO Wizard, invite your friends, and start playing.
* * *
## **Play Mode: Early Access Available Now**
In the current version of Play Mode, you can host and play single-table games with up to 9 players. You have access to three default formats: Regular, Hyper, and Cash, each with varying settings for blinds, starting stacks, and decision times. You can also customize your games by adjusting the starting stacks, blind levels, how often the blinds increase, and the decision time for each hand.
