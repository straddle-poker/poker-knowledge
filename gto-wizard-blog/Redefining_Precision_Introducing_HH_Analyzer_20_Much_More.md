****Save Reports: Streamline Your Workflow****
Save custom reports to easily revisit and analyze key areas of your game. This feature is a major time saver, allowing you to filter your data in the exact way you need—whether it’s focusing on single-raised pots, ICM situations, or preflop aggression—and then save that report for future use. Each time you upload new hand history data, you can run your saved report again to instantly analyze your latest performance without reapplying filters or starting from scratch.
For example, imagine you’ve created a report filtered to show hands where you played from the Big Blind in single-raised pots that went postflop. With your saved report, whenever you upload new hands, you can immediately see updated metrics for how you’re performing in that specific spot. This makes it easy to track your progress over time, analyze your game consistently, and ensure your study routine is as efficient as possible.
By revisiting and analyzing key areas with saved reports, you can build a consistent workflow, track improvements, and refine your strategies with precision and ease.

****PKO Study Plan****
PKOs are one of the most dynamic tournament formats, blending traditional tournament strategy with bounty-specific adjustments. This study plan guides you through essential concepts, allowing you to:
  * **Master Bounty Strategy** – Learn how to calculate bounty values accurately and adjust your hand ranges for maximum profit.
  * **Optimize Stack Management** – Navigate varying stack depths effectively to increase your expected value (EV).
  * **Excel in Bubble and Final Table Play** – Improve your Independent Chip Model (ICM) decision-making during high-pressure scenarios.
  * **Fine-Tune Postflop Adjustments** – Analyze hands to optimize aggression and capitalize on bounty opportunities.

[![gto wizard analyzer 2 start crushing pkos](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/gto-wizard-analyzer-2-start-crushing-pkos.png)](https://app.gtowizard.com/study-plans/dashboard?ref=blog.gtowizard.com)
* * *
****Spin & Go Study Plan****
Spin & Gos are fast-paced and high-variance games, making it crucial to have a solid strategy in your arsenal. Our Spin & Go Study Plan is designed to sharpen your skills and maximize your win-rate by focusing on:
  * **Effective Preflop Tactics** – Build a solid foundation with drills on heads-up small blind (HU SB), button (BTN), and 3-way play scenarios.
  * **Advanced Postflop Mastery** – Refine your defense against continuation bets (c-bets), explore sophisticated flop strategies, and master blind-versus-blind situations.
  * **Efficient Study Techniques** – Leverage GTO Wizard’s aggregate reports and prioritize key board textures to maximize your learning efficiency.
  * **Avoiding Common Mistakes** – Learn the most frequent errors made by Spin & Go players to gain a competitive edge.
