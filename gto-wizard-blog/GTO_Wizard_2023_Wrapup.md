## GTO Wizard: Progress & Plans
This past year, we’ve been at the forefront of **revolutionizing the world of poker training**. With the introduction of [GTO Wizard AI](https://blog.gtowizard.com/introducing-gto-wizard-ai/), we’ve set a new standard in the industry, offering the world’s most sophisticated poker solver. This state-of-the-art solver can instantly solve, study, and practice spots with any ranges, stack depth, and bet sizes. Our advancements didn’t stop there; we’ve integrated cutting-edge features such as [nodelocking](https://blog.gtowizard.com/introducing-nodelocking/), [heads-up preflop solving](https://blog.gtowizard.com/introducing-gto-wizard-ai-heads-up-preflop-solver/), and [customizable rake](https://blog.gtowizard.com/customizable-raked-solutions-with-gto-wizard-ai/) settings, each designed to offer a more tailored and effective learning environment. Furthermore, we established a [Fair Play Subsidiary](https://blog.gtowizard.com/towards-a-safer-poker-ecosystem). We’ve partnered with major poker operators, empowering them with the tools they need to foster a secure and trustworthy poker environment.
This past year, GTO Wizard was prominently featured in some of the most prestigious and widely followed poker tournaments, such as the World Series of Poker and the Triton Series! To see GTO Wizard recognized and utilized on the world stage is a profound honor and a moment of great significance for us. It reflects the hard work and dedication of our team and serves as a powerful motivator for us to continue innovating and pushing the boundaries.
![GTO Wizard 2023 Wrapup](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/gto-wizard-wrapup-2023-image-1.png)GTO Wizard: Proud partner of the prestigious Triton series![GTO Wizard 2023 Wrapup](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/gto-wizard-wrapup-2023-image-3.png)GTO Wizard featuring on the frontstage of the WSOP Main Event
**You can look forward to some incredible developments in 2024** as we continue to advance our state-of-the-art solver. We’ve made some breakthroughs in the domain of large-field ICM tournaments. Soon, you’ll be able to study custom ICM solutions for any payout structure and PKO bounties. Additionally, we plan to launch multiway solving, both preflop and postflop, so you’ll have access to every spot you can think of!
We’re aware, however, that all this innovation needs to be balanced to keep everybody honest. That’s why we are investing resources to bolster our Fair Play team but also keep pursuing to strengthen partnerships with poker operators so that our internal efforts really reach the poker playing community. The GTO reports we’re building, for example, will be instrumental. They will allow you to compare your stats against GTO. The fusion of lightning-fast adaptable solvers and detailed GTO reports will empower us to innovate robust game integrity algorithms.
