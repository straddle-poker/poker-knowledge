****New Solutions****
![8Max Cash Game Straddle + Ante New solutions & Improvements](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/gtowizard-8max-cashgame-straddle-ante.png)
**8Max Cash Game Straddle + Ante games** are becoming very popular, so we decided to solve this format as well! The ante is 4BB total _(0.5BB per player)_ , solved _without_ rake. We offer full preflop and postflop solutions for 100BB, 150BB, 200BB stack depths.
This is a separate tier from other formats. You’ll need a premium subscription to 8Max Cash Straddle + Ante to view the solutions _(including preflop solutions)_. Starter subscriptions are not offered for this format.
All features of GTO Wizard support this format, including the Hand history Analyzer and Practice modes. Aggregate flop reports for all depths are due to be added within one week _(Feb 9, 2022)._
To purchase the 8Max Cash game Straddle + Ante format and instantly get access to all solutions and features of GTO Wizard, please [continue here](https://app.gtowizard.com/subscription/tier-overview?gm_type=straddle_ante_cash&ref=blog.gtowizard.com).
**Simple Solutions** are now complete! Premium cash game players can now study stack depths between 20BB–200BB for both NL500 and NL50 rake structures.
We’ve also added aggregate flop reports for the NL50 Simple solutions so you can explore trends. Aggregate reports for NL500 are coming soon!
****Hand History Analyzer Improvements****
We’ve doubled the speed of the Hand history Analyzer! We aim to improve it by another 100% in the following weeks.
We now support all iPoker Spin & Go HHs, as well as hand histories for the new 8Max Cash Game Straddle + Ante format. In addition, we’ve fixed some minor bugs in reading MTT hand histories and added support for a variety of new MTT HHs. 9max MTT HHs are now supported as well _(if UTG folds)_.
If you would find a hand history that is not supported by the HH Analyzer, please reach out to us our [Discord](https://discord.gg/uuyA3BvVNv?ref=blog.gtowizard.com) server, so we can quickly add support for it!
****Normalize Hand Category Graphs in the Filters Tab****
![GTO Wizard Normalize Hand Category](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/8max-cash-game-straddle-image-2.gif)
Now you can change the way you view categories in the Filters tab! Select this icon in the top-right corner of the Filters tab to switch between _range length_ and _full length._
Range length will rescale the horizontal graphs according to their frequency within your range. This feature helps you visualize the proportional weight of each hand category. Full length is the same visualization as before, with each category scaled to 100% length.
****Weighted Equity Distribution Graph****
Previously, all combos in the equity distribution graph _(in the Ranges tab)_ were assigned the same horizontal length regardless of their weight within your range. Now we’ve weighted every combination to reflect the equity distribution more accurately. This should prevent minor distortions to the graph.
****Change Colors of All Gradients (EV, EQR, EQ, Blockers)****
![GTO Wizard Change Color](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/8max-cash-game-straddle-image-3.png)
Customize the way you view your data! With this update, you can edit the color gradient for EV, Equity, Realization _(EQR)_ , and Blocker score. Open your settings in the top-right hand corner _(_[_or continue here_](https://app.gtowizard.com/user/themes?ref=blog.gtowizard.com) _)_ , select “gradient”, and define your colors.
****Labelled Frequencies in Bet Sizing Graph****
![GTO Wizard Labelled](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/8max-cash-game-straddle-image-1.png)
Graphs are great, but graphs with numbers are better. Now you can see the frequency of different actions within the bet sizing graph. To activate a graph view, simply press “S” in the Solution browser.
****Improved Readability of the Black Font in the Range Table****
![8Max Cash Game Straddle + Ante New solutions & Improvements](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/8max-cash-game-straddle-image-4.png)![8Max Cash Game Straddle + Ante New solutions & Improvements](https://blog.gtowizard.com/content/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/8max-cash-game-straddle-image-7.png)
We’ve added a light background to active hands when using the black font in order to improve readability and prevent text from blending into the background. In case you didn’t know, we have added the ability to select the black font for your range in the previous patch, and you can activate it in the [Game settings](https://app.gtowizard.com/user/game-settings?ref=blog.gtowizard.com).
****Double-Digit EV Precision****

Some players requested more precision, so the strategy table now shows expected values to two decimal places.

Now you can toggle the loading tips. You can do so in your [Game settings](https://app.gtowizard.com/user/game-settings?ref=blog.gtowizard.com).
