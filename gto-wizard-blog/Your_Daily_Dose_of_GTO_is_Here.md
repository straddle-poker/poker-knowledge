### **MTT Solutions**
We’re excited to announce a groundbreaking update to our MTT and PKO strategy solutions, designed to help you navigate the critical late stages of large-field tournaments. This update includes a range of scenarios for 8max, 7max, and 6max tables, focusing on pivotal situations from the near-bubble stages to the final three tables, with mixed stack depths ranging from 10bb to 50bb.
  * **ICM Considerations** – Solutions for near-bubble situations provide insights on how you should leverage or protect your chip stack as you approach the money. The implications of ICM will significantly impact strategies as you progress deeper and payout jumps become more significant. These solutions will help our MTT Wizards navigate these spots more accurately.
  * **Bounty Dynamics** – In PKO formats, understanding how to adjust aggression while managing risk becomes crucial. In this update, we’ve provided key GTO solutions for late stages of PKOs, where stack sizes have a very significant impact on preflop decisions.

All of today’s MTT solutions solve for 1000-entry models. Keep in mind that [field size has a significant impact on ICM](https://blog.gtowizard.com/understanding-the-impact-of-field-size-on-icm-in-poker/), and this impact can be even greater in PKO structures. By incorporating ICM considerations and bounty dynamics, you can make higher EV decisions at the moments that impact your bankroll the most.
