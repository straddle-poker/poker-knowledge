## **New Cash Game Solutions**
We’re thrilled to introduce our newest straddle solutions, available exclusively to [**Straddle+Ante**](https://app.gtowizard.com/subscription/tier-overview?gm_type=straddle_ante_cash&ref=blog.gtowizard.com)**subscribers**. The Mississippi Straddle, often called a ‘Button straddle,’ occurs when the Button posts a third blind, forcing the action to start with the Small Blind. In this setup, both the SB and BB face a distinct disadvantage by having to act first, both preflop and postflop. Meanwhile, a Double Straddle raises the stakes further: UTG straddles, then UTG+1 re-straddles, and four blinds hit the pot before any action begins!
**Mississippi Straddle** 112 Situations _(PREFLOP)_
  * Opening sizes: 4bb, 5bb, 6bb, 7bb
  * Stack depths: 100bb, 150bb, 200bb, 300bb, 400bb, 600bb, 800bb
  * Rake structures: cEV (no rake) and Live (10% rake with 2bb cap)

**Double Straddle** 352 Situations _(PREFLOP)_
  * Opening sizes: 8bb, 10bb, 12bb, 14bb
  * Stack depths: 100bb, 200bb, 300bb, 400bb, 500bb, 600bb, 700bb, 800bb, 1000bb, 1200bb, 1600bb
  * Rake structures: cEV (no rake), NL25, NL100, NL400

These new cash game solutions introduce the high-octane dynamics of **Mississippi and Double Straddle games** directly into GTO Wizard, capturing the essence of deep stacks and oversized preflop action. With opening raises up to 14bb and stacks as deep as 1600bb, you can now precisely study scenarios that replicate the wild conditions found in live straddle games. Each scenario includes realistic live rake structures alongside chip-EV (no rake) modes, enabling you to practice with authentic conditions. Whether you’re gearing up for casual home games or high-stakes casino action, these solutions will sharpen your ranges and help you dominate challenging straddle pots.
