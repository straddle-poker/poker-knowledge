## **Why Is This a Game Changer?**
Most tournament players study with Chip EV solutions. Chip EV strategies aim to accumulate as many chips as possible, ignoring the value of survival. The {{tooltip-title: Independent Chip Model}}, on the other hand, **considers the payout implications of the entire tournament**. ICM strategies maximize the actual cash value of your strategy, leading to dramatically different GTO strategies that give you a significant edge in your game.
{{tooltip-content: Independent Chip Model}}
**ICM (Independent Chip Model)**  
A commonly used acronym for the term Independent Chip Model. ICM is a model that translates the value of a tournament chip to actual cash value, better known as tournament equity. ICM calculations are based on the stacks and payouts of all players remaining in the tournament.
{{/tooltip-content}}
Traditionally, studying an ICM spot required multiple pieces of software, extensive computing power, and considerable time to solve just one individual spot. This cumbersome process made it challenging to study effectively and efficiently. With our new update, you can solve these spots in mere seconds.
