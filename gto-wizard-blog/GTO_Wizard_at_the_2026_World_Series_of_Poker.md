Speaking to PokerNews during the series, Mullur was unequivocal about the role GTO Wizard plays in his game:
> "It is the easiest solver to use, and also the best and biggest library on the market. I use it every day, all day basically, and I think it helps a lot with preparation. They are making some really good and smart business moves, and it is a big part of my studying."

He is joined by Shiina Okamoto, the reigning two-time WSOP Ladies Championship winner and one of the most accomplished players in the field. The Tokyo-born pro has been a dominant force in the event since 2023, when she finished runner-up before capturing back-to-back titles and bracelets in 2024 and 2025.
