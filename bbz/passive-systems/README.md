> **Provenance:** reconstructed from public sources — NOT course transcripts. The module structure follows the public [BBZ product page](https://bbzpoker.com/product/passive-poker-systems/); strategy content is grounded in the cited articles (GTO Wizard blog, Upswing Poker, BBZ blog). Passive Poker Systems — the checked-pot layer of the BBZ systems ecosystem. Where [Simple Poker Systems](../systems/) covered you as the bettor and [Pressure Systems](../pressure-systems/) covered you as the aggressor, this course fills the gaps: heads-up pots after the action goes passive — check-back flops, probe bets, and river play after every passive sequence. Eight human-executable systems (PS1–PS8) in the S-series/P-series format: buckets, decision rules, sizings, hand examples, quiz spots and Q&A.

## Study path

1. **PS1 → PS2** form a matched pair (probing vs defending the probe) — study together.
2. **PS3 → PS7** walk the river geometries: XC-X (caller side), c-bet-X (bettor side), XR-X (check-raiser side), XX-XX OOP and IP.
3. **PS8** closes the loop
: the four constant questions (capping, nut advantage, card classes, blockers) and how stack depth, ICM and format edit the sizing ladder without changing the shapes.

## Core heuristics

- "The check-back caps them; the turn card tells you how much" (PS1)
- "Bluff-catching at MDF — raise is the rarest tool from a capped range" (PS2)
- "OOP nut hands check-raise; they don't build overbet ranges" (PS3/PS5/PS6)
- "IP rivers: big or check — small bets donate the option" (PS7)
- "No nuts advantage, no lead" (PS6)
- "Four questions, every format: capped? nuts? card class? blockers?" (PS8)
